03 / Under the hood

What this prototype actually runs

Concrete computational modules, with research limitations separated from engineering checks.

1. Compound graph & descriptors

RDKit parses canonical isomeric SMILES, computes molecular descriptors and prepares Morgan radius-2 fingerprints for the adapter. The molecule graphic is generated from the input, not pre-rendered.

2. Protein sequence + geometry

Saved ESM-2 residue embeddings feed the frozen sequence predictor. An existing mapped N/CA/C/O protein backbone graph enables the GVP geometry branch for one of four supported targets.

3. Same-assay support adaptation

Selected DAVIS development support examples calibrate the model using similarity-weighted measured pKd. K=0 disables calibration. Predictions are not experimentally verified binding constants.
